Game overview

Two card games, one club

OWL IN is a multiplayer card-game website for the local network. Pick a name, get $10,000 KSU Dining Dollars (play money) and sit at live tables with other people and AI bots.

Texas Hold'em

  • 8 seats, blinds and a moving button. No-limit betting, side pots.
  • Your clock: +20 s each turn, up to 120 s. At 0 you auto check or fold.
  • Ready up between hands. Busted? Reload or cash out.

Blackjack

  • 7 spots, up to 3 per player. 6-deck shoe, dealer stands on all 17s.
  • Blackjack pays 3:2. Double, split, insurance, late surrender.
  • One shoe for many rounds, with a cut card; every card is dealt face up.

AI players

  • Bots fill empty seats: 4 per poker table, spots 1–3 in blackjack.
  • Some bots are Claude: an LLM that sees only what its seat sees.
  • Sit down and you replace the bot whose stack is closest to your buy-in.

  • Streets advance when every player still in has acted and the bets are level. If everyone else folds, the hand jumps straight to Hand over.
  • Hand over → Pre-flop waits for every human to ready up (5 s grace, then their clock runs). Bot-only tables deal once a minute.
State machine · blackjack table

Where every blackjack table is right now

LiveServer offline · sample data
  • Place bets is a 15 s window that closes early once everyone has pressed Deal. A dealer ace opens an 8 s insurance window first.
  • Round over shows results for 3 s, sweeps the cards to the discard tray, reshuffles past the cut card and opens betting again.

      Honest notes
      Limitations and challenges
      
  
      
    
      
      
      Limitations
      
      
        
        
      • Everything lives in memory. Restarting the server resets bankrolls, careers and names.
        • 
        
      • The HootBox only works on the server PC in Chrome or Edge: Web Serial needs localhost (or HTTPS).
        • 
        
      • Blackjack bots aren't AI yet. They play basic strategy; only the poker bots use the LLM.
        • 
        
      • AI think time is 2–6 s per decision, and every call costs a little money.
        • 
      
      
    
      
    
      
      
      Challenges
      
      
        
        
      • Browsers froze after a few table visits: every page made a new WebGL context. Fix: one shared PixiJS app.
        • 
        
      • Fair AI: the LLM gets exactly the seat's view. Hidden cards never leave the server.
        • 
        
      • Every chip accounted for across bankrolls, tables, bots and the house bank, checked live.
        • 
        
      • Timing clocks and ready-up around animations so nobody loses time watching chips fly.
        •
